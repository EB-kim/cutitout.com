Privacy policy

Last updated:

The short version

Your images never leave your device. Everything the tool does — background removal, cutting, erasing, exporting — happens locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or see your images. We have no accounts and ask for no personal information.

Your images

When you open an image in the tool, it is loaded into your browser's memory and drawn to a canvas on your device. It is discarded when you close or refresh the tab. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

Information we collect

We do not collect names, emails, or accounts. Like most websites, our host and content-delivery network may process standard technical data (such as your IP address and browser type) to serve the page and protect against abuse. We do not use this to identify you.

Advertising and cookies

This site is supported by ads served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Ad scripts are loaded from Google's ad domains. These scripts are separate from our own code — our "your image never leaves your device" promise applies to how we handle your images, not to Google's advertising systems.

Analytics

If we use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand page traffic, it does not identify individual users. We do not sell any data.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
