Privacy

cutitoutbg.com runs entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

No uploads

Every image you open, cut, refine, and export is processed locally by your own browser. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

Nothing is stored

Images and pieces exist only in your browser's memory while this page is open. Closing or refreshing the tab erases everything — there is no database, no cloud, and no account.

No tracking

By default this page sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or analytics.

Optional AI cutout

Only if you click \u201cAI cutout\u201d, the app downloads an open-source AI model (BiRefNet, MIT-licensed) from a public CDN (jsDelivr / Hugging Face) to your browser the first time, then caches it. Your image is still processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded \u2014 only the model is fetched.

Exports stay with you

"Export PNG" builds the file in your browser and saves it straight to your device.

Because everything happens on your device, we never see your images and could not retrieve them even if asked.
