Remove a background without uploading anything

Most background removers send your photo to a server. Cut It Out doesn't. Everything — loading, erasing, refining, and exporting — happens inside your browser using the HTML canvas. There is no account to create and no file to upload, which means your picture is never stored, logged, or seen by us. When you close the tab, it's gone.

How the manual cutout works

  1. Drop your image. Drag it onto the editor or click to pick a file.
  2. Magic Wand. Click a background area and connected pixels of a similar color are erased. Raise the tolerance for gradients and shadows; lower it for fine detail.
  3. Eraser & Restore. Paint away leftover fringes, or restore pixels you removed by mistake.
  4. Export. Save a transparent PNG, or flatten onto a white background if you need a solid backdrop.

When manual beats one-click

Automatic AI removers are great on clean portraits but stumble on screenshots, logos on white, signatures, product shots with clutter, and fine hair. A quick manual pass gives you precise control the automatic tools can't — and it's instant, with nothing to download.
