✂️ How to use CutItOut

Cut parts out of one image, gather them onto a new canvas, and export on white or transparent. Everything runs in your browser — your image is never uploaded.

Step 1 · Cut mode — isolate a part

  1. Add an image: drag it in, click Choose Image, or paste from your clipboard.
  2. Pick a part: click Box-Select a Part (AI) and drag a rough box around the object — the AI cuts it out cleanly along its real edges.
  3. Or use Auto Remove Background for the whole subject, or Magic Erase / Erase / Restore brushes for manual control.
  4. Click ➕ Add this part to collage. The image resets so you can grab another part from the same photo.

Step 2 · Combine mode — arrange & export

  1. Switch to 🖼️ Combine at the top.
  2. Move a part by dragging it · resize with the corner dot · rotate with the top dot.
  3. Use To Front / To Back / Flip / Delete to fine-tune each part.
  4. Choose White or Transparent background, then click ⬇ Export Collage.
💡 Free & private: the AI runs on your own device — no server, no signup, no per-image cost. The first AI use downloads a small model once, then it's cached. You can also just cut one image and hit Download PNG without making a collage.
